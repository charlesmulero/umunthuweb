Our SDG Interventions

Umunthu Lead contributes to the Sustainable Development Goals by addressing interconnected social, economic, environmental and community challenges through youth-led action and community engagement.

1

No Poverty

Supporting vulnerable communities and young people through livelihoods, entrepreneurship, skills development and resilience-building initiatives.

2

Zero Hunger

Supporting food security and resilient livelihoods through community initiatives that promote sustainable food production, access to nutritious food and household resilience.

3

Good Health and Well-being

Promoting community health, mental well-being, healthy lifestyles and access to health information and support.

4

Quality Education

Creating opportunities for learning, skills development, digital literacy, mentorship and inclusive education.

5

Gender Equality

Advancing gender equality and addressing gender-based violence, child marriage, teenage pregnancy and other inequalities affecting women and girls.

10

Reduced Inequalities

Promoting inclusion and participation of marginalized and vulnerable groups, including people with disabilities and underserved communities.

13

Climate Action

Promoting environmental awareness, climate action, clean energy and community resilience to environmental challenges.

16

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Strengthening peace, social cohesion, human rights, civic participation, accountability and access to social justice.

17

Partnerships for the Goals

Building partnerships among communities, young people, civil society, government, private sector and other stakeholders to advance sustainable development
