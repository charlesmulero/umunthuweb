Key Areas of Focus

1

Strengthening Social Cohesion, Peacebuilding & Inclusion

Umunthu Lead promotes peaceful and inclusive communities through dialogue, social justice, human rights protection, civic participation, arts, cultural exchange, leadership development, and community engagement. We work to prevent violence, discrimination, and exclusion, including gender-based violence, violent extremism, tribalism, xenophobia, hate speech, and other forms of social division while promoting unity and respect for diversity.

2

Community Development, Health & Empowerment

We support communities to improve their quality of life by expanding access to education, healthcare, livelihoods, skills development, entrepreneurship, and essential services. Our work includes health and nutrition awareness, mental health support, child healthcare, community empowerment, and Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) initiatives that strengthen community wellbeing and resilience.

3

Humanitarian Action

Umunthu Lead provides support to vulnerable communities affected by emergencies, disasters, and crises through humanitarian assistance, protection services, recovery support, and resilience-building initiatives. We focus on protecting dignity, supporting affected populations, and strengthening community capacity to respond to future challenges, including child protection and safeguarding.

4

Environmental Stewardship

We promote environmental responsibility through climate resilience, conservation, renewable energy, sustainable practices, and community-led solutions. Our work supports communities to protect natural resources, strengthen livelihoods, and adopt environmentally sustainable approaches for present and future generations.
