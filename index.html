About Us

Umunthu Lead is a youth-led non-governmental organization founded in 2023 in Malawi, committed to building a peaceful, inclusive, and thriving society where communities are empowered to actively participate in their own development.

Inspired by the African philosophy of “Umunthu” — the belief that “I am because we are”, the organization recognizes that sustainable progress is achieved through collective responsibility, compassion, dignity, and solidarity among people.
